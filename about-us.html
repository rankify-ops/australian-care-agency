Making Lives Better Every Day

Our Mission:

At Australian Care Agency, our mission is simple: to make lives better. We are dedicated to providing exceptional, personalized NDIS Home Care Packages and support that empowers individuals to live independently with dignity and confidence. Our compassionate, highly trained team of support workers is passionate about promoting independence and enhancing the quality of life for every person we serve.

As a proud, registered provider with both the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) and the Transport Accident Commission (TAC), we are committed to delivering the highest standards of care across Australia. Our services are tailored to meet the unique needs of each individual, ensuring that every client receives the support they need, when they need it most.

Our Vision:

We believe in the power of care to transform lives. Our vision is to provide support that allows individuals to remain independent in their own homes and communities, surrounded by familiar comforts while receiving professional and compassionate assistance.
