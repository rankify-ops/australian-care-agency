NDIS Meal Prep

At Australian Care Agency, we recognize that nutritious, home-cooked meals are essential for maintaining health and well-being. Our Meal Preparation services are designed to provide practical and reliable support with planning, shopping, and preparing meals that are tailored to each individual’s dietary needs, preferences, and goals.

From creating meal plans that align with specific health requirements to assisting with grocery shopping and cooking, our team is here to make mealtime stress-free and enjoyable.

Our Meal Preparation service goes beyond simply cooking. We work collaboratively with participants to encourage independence in the kitchen where possible, offering support that builds confidence and skills.

We also place a strong emphasis on dietary considerations, including allergies, intolerances, cultural preferences, and specific health goals.
