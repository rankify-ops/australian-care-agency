NDIS Laundry Service

At Australian Care Agency, we understand the importance of clean and fresh clothing in maintaining personal comfort and dignity. Our NDIS Laundry Services are designed to provide reliable and professional assistance with all your laundry needs, ensuring participants can enjoy the confidence and convenience of properly managed laundry.

Whether it’s washing, drying, folding, or ironing, our dedicated team handles each task with care and attention to detail.

Our services are tailored to meet the unique needs of each participant, offering flexibility and convenience.

In addition to household laundry, we can also assist with bed linens, towels, and other textiles, ensuring your entire home feels fresh and well-kept.
